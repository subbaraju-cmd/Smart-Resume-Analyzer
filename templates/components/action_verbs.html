Action Verbs to Use

Keywords
Category Recommended Verbs
System Design & Architecture Architected, Engineered, Formulated, Designed, Structured, Modeled, Configured
Execution & Delivery Implemented, Developed, Deployed, Built, Integrated, Orchestrated, Executed
Optimization & Performance Accelerated, Optimized, Streamlined, Refactored, Scaled, Automated, Reduced
Data & Analytics Analyzed, Extracted, Processed, Transformed, Visualized, Validated, Evaluated

Formatting Mistakes to Avoid

  • • Multi-column layouts: Causes text stream disruption in legacy parsers. Use a clean single-column format.
  • • Graphic Skill Bars: Visual rating bars (e.g. 4/5 stars) cannot be read by ATS software.
  • • Header/Footer Text: Do not place phone, email, or links inside page margin headers/footers.
